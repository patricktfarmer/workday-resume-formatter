🤔 Asking “why the hell do I need this?”
📱 Works great on your phone. The two panels just stack top-to-bottom instead of side-by-side. Your resume is on top, the form below it. Tap ⇆ Swap sides to flip which one is on top.

Your resume

📄 Drop your resume here or

Accepts .pdf, .docx, .txt. Your information never leaves your device; it all stays inside your own browser.

💡 Copy whole sections, not line by line. To fill in your Achievements, select the entire bulleted section from your resume and paste it into the Achievements box in one go. Your Skills work the same way. Even if it looks messy or bunched up when you copy it out of a PDF, we clean it up and split it into proper lines for you.

How to use this: the two-file trick

  1. Upload the file you make here first. It's built so Workday's parser reads your details correctly and autofills the form, so you're not retyping everything by hand.
  2. Let it fill the form, then fix anything the drop-downs need (your school, maybe your degree).
  3. At the end of the work-experience page, Workday shows your uploaded file with a delete option. Delete it and re-upload your nice, human-friendly resume, the one you spent time on. That becomes the copy the recruiter actually reads, and it won't change what was already filled in.

We know you spent time on your resume, and that's what the recruiter gets. This version is just to hand the system your details cleanly and save you the typing.
